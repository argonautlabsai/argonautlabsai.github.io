WEBSITE PRIVACY · 9 NOVEMBER 2026

Your choice about analytics.

You can read our research, use our guides and visit our repositories without allowing analytics. Use to change your choice at any time.

What we measure

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand daily visitors, pages read, referral sources and clicks to our GitHub projects. Google processes technical information such as browser and device information, approximate geography and cookie identifiers to produce these reports. These measurements are not a complete count of everyone who visits: people who decline, block tracking or request privacy protection are excluded.

Our GitHub click event records the repository, the type of destination (such as a release or source page), and where the link appeared on our website. It measures a click from this site, not whether you subsequently download, star or use a repository.

The analytics reports are access-restricted to the website owner’s Google account. We do not publish visitor records or a public analytics dashboard. Google processes the analytics data as the service provider.

What we leave out

Our tracking code uses the page’s canonical address without query strings or fragments, and reduces referral addresses to their origin. GitHub destinations are reduced to the repository address. We do not send names, email addresses, messages, model prompts, file paths from your computer or form contents. We do not enable Google Signals, advertising storage or personalised advertising.

Your choice and cookies

Before you opt in, we do not load Google Analytics or send it analytics events. Your allow or decline choice is saved in your browser for up to 180 days. If you opt in, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_…, configured to expire after 180 days without automatically extending their expiry.

Declining later stops analytics, removes those cookies on this site and reloads the page to unload the analytics code. Withdrawing permission does not erase information already sent. A Global Privacy Control signal or Do Not Track setting keeps analytics off.

Hosting and other websites

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Delivering a page necessarily involves technical requests to its hosting infrastructure, independently of our optional analytics. GitHub and Google have their own data practices. Links to GitHub, Hugging Face and other sites take you to services governed by their own policies.
