More drives help.
Placement matters too.

In the 128-token drafted test, the internal SSD alone delivered about 52% of the four-drive throughput. Two drives delivered 73%; three delivered 90%.

The smaller generation rungs are single runs. The one- and two-drive layouts use full mirrors; the three- and four-drive layouts use usage-weighted placement.

What these results establish

This is a single-machine benchmark, not a general speed guarantee. Experts retain their released MXFP4 precision; the resident trunk is int8. Output identity checks apply to the tested prompts and configurations, not every prompt or storage layout. Long-context and interactive-chat performance are not established by these tests.
